Key Insights:

- Email Marketing is the strongest channel overall: highest revenue, lowest ad spend, highest conversions, and highest ROAS

- Google Ads is a solid second, with good revenue and conversions but a noticeably higher spend, resulting in lower ROAS than Email Marketing
  
- Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok trail behind both in revenue and ROAS
  
- By country: Saudi Arabia is the only High-performing market, Bahrain is Low, and the rest fall in the Medium range
